Flagship project

Customer Retention & Transaction Analytics

  • Rebuilt the same analysis independently in Excel, MySQL, Python and Power BI, and cross-checked every core KPI across all four tools.
  • Modeled 24,244 transactions, 7,000 customers and 6,035 support tickets (Jan 2024 to Dec 2025) in a 5-table star schema.

Flagship project

Global Supply Chain & Delivery Performance Analytics

  • Designed a star-schema database in MySQL (5 dimension and 2 fact tables) from a 53-column flat file, covering 180K+ orders, 5 markets and 118 products.
  • Found a 54.83% late-delivery rate, driven by First Class shipping (95.3% late versus 38.1% for Standard Class).
  • Flagged 33,784 loss-making line items (18.7%), and showed profit margin is independent of sales volume (Fishing is #1 in sales but ranks about #29 of ~50 on margin).
  • Quantified $744K in cancelled sales and confirmed risk is spread evenly across regions, segments and shipping modes, not concentrated.
  • Matched every headline KPI across MySQL, Python and Power BI, and used scatter and box plots to explain the margin findings.
  • Built a 3-page Power BI dashboard with slicers, DAX and managed table relationships.
